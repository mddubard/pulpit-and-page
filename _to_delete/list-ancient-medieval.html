The Ancient & Medieval World

by The Picky Reader

The ancient and medieval world, told by writers worth the hours — Rome and its fall, Byzantium, and the long, inventive centuries we still call the Middle Ages. History as narrative, not textbook.

1.

S.P.Q.R.: A History of Ancient Rome

Mary Beard · Liveright

A witty, questioning history of Rome that trades triumphal myth for how Romans actually lived, argued, and imagined themselves into an empire. Buy on Bookshop →

2.

Rubicon: The Last Years of the Roman Republic

Tom Holland · Vintage

Holland turns the Republic's slow collapse into a gripping political thriller of ambition and civil war — prose so vivid it reads like the best fiction. Buy on Bookshop →

3.

Powers and Thrones: A New History of the Middle Ages

Dan Jones · Penguin Books

A sweeping, propulsive thousand-year tour from Rome's fall to the Reformation, told with color and momentum that makes the medieval world feel alive. Buy on Bookshop →

4.

The Inheritance of Rome: Illuminating the Dark Ages, 400–1000

Chris Wickham · Penguin Books

A masterwork that dismantles the myth of the 'Dark Ages,' showing an inventive post-Roman world on its own terms, with rigor and clarity. Buy on Bookshop →

5.

Byzantium: The Surprising Life of a Medieval Empire

Judith Herrin · Princeton University Press

An elegant portrait of the empire that carried classical civilization for a millennium — full of icons, empresses, and unexpected human detail. Buy on Bookshop →

6.

A Distant Mirror: The Calamitous 14th Century

Barbara W. Tuchman · Random House

Tuchman's Pulitzer-winning storytelling follows one French nobleman through plague, war, and upheaval, making a whole shattering century intimate. Buy on Bookshop →

7.

Through the Eye of a Needle

Peter Brown · Princeton University Press

The dean of late antiquity on how money, faith, and a dying empire reshaped the Western world — magisterial and beautifully written. Buy on Bookshop →
