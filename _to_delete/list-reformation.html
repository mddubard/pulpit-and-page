Church History

The Reformation & the Reformers

by The Picky Reader

The books that make sense of the sixteenth century's great rupture — the lives of Luther and Calvin, the movement they set loose, and the world it remade. A focused companion to Essential Church History.

Shop these on Bookshop →

Every purchase supports independent bookstores.

1.

Here I Stand: A Life of Martin Luther

Roland H. Bainton · Penguin

The classic Luther biography, still unmatched for warmth and drama — Bainton makes the monk who defied an empire feel vividly, stubbornly human. Buy on Bookshop →

2.

Calvin

Bruce Gordon · Yale University Press

The finest modern life of Calvin — Gordon renders the brilliant, embattled Geneva reformer with rare balance, neither hagiography nor hit piece. Buy on Bookshop →

3.

Brand Luther

Andrew Pettegree · Penguin

A fresh, ingenious angle: how Luther and the new printing press remade each other, turning a provincial monk into Europe's first mass-media celebrity. Buy on Bookshop →

4.

The Reformation: How a Monk and a Mallet Changed the World

Stephen J. Nichols · Crossway

A brisk, engaging introduction for newcomers — Nichols tells the whole story with wit and clarity, keeping the theology front and center. Buy on Bookshop →

5.

Christianity's Dangerous Idea

Alister McGrath · HarperOne

McGrath traces one explosive idea — that ordinary believers can read Scripture for themselves — across five centuries of Protestant vitality and division. Buy on Bookshop →

6.

Reformations: The Early Modern World, 1450–1650

Carlos M. N. Eire · Yale University Press

Panoramic and elegantly written, attentive to Catholics and radicals alike — arguably the best single-volume account of the whole era. Buy on Bookshop →
