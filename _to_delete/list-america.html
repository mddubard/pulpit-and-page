American History

America's Story

by The Picky Reader

The American story in the hands of its best storytellers — the founding, the Civil War, and the struggles that shaped a nation, plus the faith that ran through it. Narrative history, chosen for the writing.

Every purchase supports independent bookstores.

1.

These Truths: A History of the United States

Jill Lepore · W. W. Norton

A dazzling single-volume sweep from 1492 to today, written with a novelist's eye and organized around the nation's founding ideals — and its failures to keep them. Buy on Bookshop →

2.

1776

David McCullough · Simon & Schuster

McCullough follows Washington's ragged army through a single desperate year of the Revolution, in prose so vivid you feel the cold and the cannon smoke. Buy on Bookshop →

3.

Founding Brothers: The Revolutionary Generation

Joseph J. Ellis · Vintage

A Pulitzer winner that brings the founders to life through six intimate episodes — Jefferson, Adams, Hamilton, and the rest, brilliant and quarreling. Buy on Bookshop →

4.

Battle Cry of Freedom: The Civil War Era

James M. McPherson · Oxford University Press

Widely hailed as the finest one-volume history of the Civil War — a Pulitzer winner that reads like a page-turner without sacrificing depth. Buy on Bookshop →

5.

Team of Rivals: The Political Genius of Abraham Lincoln

Doris Kearns Goodwin · Simon & Schuster

How Lincoln outmaneuvered and then befriended his fiercest rivals — a masterclass in leadership, told with warmth and momentum. Buy on Bookshop →

6.

The Warmth of Other Suns

Isabel Wilkerson · Vintage

Through three unforgettable lives, Wilkerson chronicles the six million who fled the Jim Crow South — a landmark written with luminous prose. Buy on Bookshop →

7.

The Evangelicals: The Struggle to Shape America

Frances FitzGerald · Simon & Schuster

A Pulitzer-winning, evenhanded account of American evangelicalism from the Great Awakenings on — essential for understanding faith in the nation's public life. Buy on Bookshop →
