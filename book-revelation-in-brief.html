Unveiling Jesus in the Bible's Final Book

About the book

Leithart reads Revelation as a connected story about Jesus and the early church, offering five interpretive principles and insisting its symbols point to real people and events rooted in the rest of Scripture. A contested figure in Reformed circles, but few writers hear Old Testament echoes as well, and this short introduction shows it.
