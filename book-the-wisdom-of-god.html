The Wisdom of God

Matthew S. Harmon

Crossway · Biblical Studies · 144 pages

From the publisher

In this addition to the Short Studies in Biblical Theology series, Matthew S. Harmon explores God's wisdom throughout Scripture from Eden to the new creation, finding its fullest expression in the person of Christ.

Description adapted from the publisher, Crossway.

Endorsements

"In this short, wise, and accessible book, Matthew Harmon introduces us to the way wisdom works in Scripture, how people respond to it, and what God does next. This book is a clear and concise introduction to an important (and often neglected) theme."

— Andrew Wilson, Teaching Pastor, King's Church London

Endorsements published by the publisher; more on the publisher's page.

About the author. Matthew S. Harmon (PhD, Wheaton College) is professor of New Testament Studies at Grace College and Theological Seminary in Winona Lake, Indiana. He is the author of numerous books, including commentaries on Galatians, Philippians, 2 Peter, and Jude.
