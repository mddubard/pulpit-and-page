Publishers We Track

Every week we watch the new-release feeds of these houses, across the Reformed, evangelical, Baptist, Anglican, and classic-Christian world, so you don't have to.

Reformed & Presbyterian

Crossway

ESV Bibles and gospel-centered books; biblical & systematic theology, Christian living.

P&R Publishing

Confessional Presbyterian & Reformed — the Reformed Expository Commentary and much more.

Banner of Truth

Puritan and Reformed classics and new works in that stream.

Reformation Heritage Books

Puritan reprints and Reformed theology; the home of Joel Beeke's work.

Christian Focus / Mentor

Scottish Reformed publisher; Mentor is its academic imprint.

Reformation Trust (Ligonier)

R. C. Sproul and the Ligonier stable of Reformed teachers.

EP Books

Evangelical Press — accessible Reformed titles.

Davenant Press

Protestant "retrieval" theology from the Davenant Institute.

Evangelical & Academic

IVP / IVP Academic

Broad evangelical scholarship — theology, biblical studies, spiritual formation.

Eerdmans

Major academic house; biblical studies, theology, and patristics.

Zondervan Academic

Seminary textbooks, counterpoints volumes, biblical languages.

Baker Academic

Faith and scholarship for students and pastors (now including Lexham Academic titles).

Lexham Press

Logos-connected; strong on Bible reference and trade titles.

The Good Book Company

Gospel-centered popular books and Bible studies.

New Growth Press

CCEF-connected biblical counseling and gospel-centered resources.

Baptist

B&H Academic

Southern Baptist scholarly publishing.

Founders Press

Historic (Reformed) Baptist principles — confessions and doctrine.

Free Grace Press

Reformed Baptist, confessional books.

H&E Publishing

Reformed Baptist titles and reprints.
