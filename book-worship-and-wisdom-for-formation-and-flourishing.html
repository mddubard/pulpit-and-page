Worship and Wisdom for Formation and Flourishing

Ryan J. Cook

Kregel Academic · Biblical Studies
Published August 2026 · ISBN 9780825453779

Buy at Amazon Christianbook Kregel Academic

From the publisher

Walking faithfully with God cannot be separated from humans' deepest emotional and spiritual needs. The ancient authors and compilers of Psalms, Job, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, and Song of Songs consciously addressed this reality in their time. Worship and Wisdom for Formation and Flourishing introduces Bible students to these rich Old Testament books.

Description adapted from the publisher, Kregel Academic.

About the author. Ryan J. Cook (PhD, Asbury Theological Seminary) is the dean of faculty and professor of Old Testament and Hebrew at Moody Theological Seminary. He lives in northwest Indiana.
