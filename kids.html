How we choose

Every book on this shelf is checked before it goes up. We read the publisher's description, trade reviews, and reader reviews, and for a series, we look at the earlier books too. We also check that each book fits the age it's sold for.

We leave off

  • Romance, including teen romance, and books where a love story drives the plot
  • Books with sexual content, or whose subject is sexuality or gender identity
  • Books that make disrespect for parents the message, or crude humor the point
  • Books that present real-world occult practice as good, or that mock religious faith
  • Violence or frightening content beyond what fits the age
  • Profanity in books for younger readers, and constant profanity in any book

Children's books can still deal with real things like loss, danger, and doing wrong. Fairy-tale magic, villains, and a little silliness are fine. When a book has something worth knowing about, we say so in a content note on its page.

Many of these books are new or not yet out, so our notes are based on what has been published about them.

If you read one and think we missed something, write to us at info@thepickyreader.com.
