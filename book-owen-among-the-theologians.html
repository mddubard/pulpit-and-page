From the publisher

In Owen Among the Theologians, authors Kelly M. Kapic and Ty Kieser invite readers to explore the theology of John Owen alongside the voices of other influential figures throughout church history.

Description adapted from the publisher, Crossway.

Endorsements

"The recent revival of interest in the life and work of John Owen allows us to see how the greatest theologian in seventeenth-century England speaks to concerns from across the history of the church. Written by two premier Owen scholars, this book offers a first-class account."

— Crawford Gribben, Professor of Early Modern British History, Queen's University Belfast

Endorsements published by the publisher; more on the publisher's page.

About the author. Kelly M. Kapic (PhD, King's College, University of London) holds the honorary chair of theology and culture at Covenant College in Lookout Mountain, Georgia, where he has taught since 2001. Kapic is an author or editor of more than fifteen books.
