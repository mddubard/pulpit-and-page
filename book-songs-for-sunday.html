About the book

A short, practical book on choosing songs and hymns for Sunday worship and solving the problems that come with it, aimed at solo pastors, church planters and new worship leaders. Kelly teaches music at West Chester University and directs music at a Presbyterian church, so the counsel comes from both the classroom and the pew.
