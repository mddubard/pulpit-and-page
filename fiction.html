How we choose

Every book on this shelf is checked before it goes up. We read the publisher's description, trade reviews, and reader reviews, and for a series, we look at the earlier books too.

We leave off

  • Romance novels, and books where the love story drives the plot
  • Books with explicit sexual content
  • Books that glamorize cruelty or wrongdoing, or that mock religious faith
  • Graphic violence or gore for its own sake
  • Books with constant profanity

Good fiction doesn't pretend the world is clean. Some books here deal honestly with war, crime, grief, or evil. When a book has violence, dark themes, or some language, we say so in a content note on its page.

Many of these books are new or not yet out, so our notes are based on what has been published about them.

If you read one and think we missed something, write to us at info@thepickyreader.com.
