From the publisher

Discover the depth, beauty, and enduring power of historic Reformed worship. In The Worship of the Reformed Church, esteemed pastor and scholar Terry L. Johnson presents a comprehensive, compelling case for recovering the biblical, theological, and historical foundations of Reformed worship in our contemporary age.

With clarity and conviction, Johnson demonstrates how Scripture-saturated, God-centered, gospel-structured worship not only shaped centuries of Christian piety but remains essential for the church today. Blending biblical exposition, historical insight, and pastoral wisdom, this substantial volume is a guide to worship that is ordered by the word and directed to the glory of God.

Description adapted from the publisher, Christian Focus (Mentor).

About the author. Terry L. Johnson is the senior minister of Independent Presbyterian Church in Savannah, Georgia, where he has served since 1987. He is the author of many books on worship and the Christian life, including Reformed Worship, The Identity and Attributes of God, and Serving with Calvin.
