Union with Christ and the New Covenant

Joshua Heavin

Baker Academic · Biblical Theology

Buy at Amazon Bookshop

About the book

Heavin examines Paul's language of dying, being buried and rising with Christ, arguing that it forms a coherent covenantal program rooted in Second Temple Judaism, and surveys fifty years of debate along the way. A demanding monograph for students and pastors who want union with Christ grounded in exegesis rather than slogans.
