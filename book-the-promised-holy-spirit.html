About the book

Teer sets out a doctrine of the Holy Spirit framed by classical trinitarian categories, including inseparable operations and the divine missions, and tied closely to the work of Christ. A 216-page volume in Studies in Historical and Systematic Theology, by a professor at Dallas Theological Seminary.
