Church History

The Ancient & Medieval Church

by The Picky Reader

The first fifteen centuries, in the order we’d hand them to someone. Two primary sources to begin with, then the historians — and a deliberate mix of the ancient church and the medieval world that grew out of it.

Shop these on Bookshop →

Every purchase supports independent bookstores.

1.

On the Incarnation

Athanasius · trans. CSMV, introduction by C. S. Lewis

Start here. Fourth-century Alexandria on why God became man, and Lewis’s introduction is itself a small classic on why old books are easier than we fear. Buy on Bookshop →

2.

Confessions

Augustine · trans. F. J. Sheed · Bloomsbury

The book that invented the interior life as a subject. Sheed’s translation keeps Augustine’s restlessness intact rather than smoothing it into piety. Buy on Bookshop →

3.

Reading the Bible with Ten Church Fathers

Gerald Bray · Baker Academic

How the early church actually read Scripture — ten figures, their habits of interpretation, and why they sound so unlike a modern commentary. See the book →

4.

The Spirit of Early Christian Thought

Robert Louis Wilken · Yale University Press

Wilken’s argument is that the fathers were not building a system but trying to change lives. The warmest book on this list, and the best on what they were actually after. Buy on Bookshop →

5.

The Church in Ancient Society

Henry Chadwick · Oxford University Press

Six centuries from Galilee to Gregory the Great, by the historian who knew the period best. The reference volume — dense, but you will keep returning to it. Buy on Bookshop →

6.

Ecclesiastical History of the English People

Bede · trans. Leo Sherley-Price · Penguin Classics

A Northumbrian monk writing in 731, and the reason we know anything about the English church before him. Primary source that reads like a story. Buy on Bookshop →

7.

The Rise of Western Christendom

Peter Brown · Wiley-Blackwell

The bridge between the two halves of this list — AD 200 to 1000, by the historian who invented “late antiquity” as a field. Shows the church becoming Europe. Buy on Bookshop →

8.

The Making of the Middle Ages

R. W. Southern · Yale University Press

Sixty years old and still the way in. Southern on the tenth to thirteenth centuries, and on what medieval people were like from the inside. Buy on Bookshop →

9.

Bernard of Clairvaux

ed. Gwenfair Walters Adams · B&H Academic · Nov 2026

Bernard in his own words, newly collected — the abbot who shaped medieval devotion and whom Calvin quoted more warmly than almost any other. See the book →
