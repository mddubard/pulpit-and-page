Exegetical Fallacies

D. A. Carson & Andrew David Naselli

Baker Academic · Biblical Studies
Published August 2026 · ISBN 9781540969224

Buy at Amazon Christianbook Baker Academic

From the publisher

D. A. Carson and Andrew David Naselli teach interpreters to think critically and to develop sound reasons for the choices they make. To do this, interpreters must reject unsound reasons; so the authors identify common mistakes many Bible interpreters make and how to avoid them.

Description adapted from the publisher, Baker Academic.

About the authors. Andrew David Naselli (PhD, Bob Jones University; PhD, Trinity Evangelical Divinity School) is professor of systematic theology and New Testament at Bethlehem College and Seminary in Minneapolis and lead pastor of Christ the King Church in Stillwater, Minnesota. He is the author of several books.
