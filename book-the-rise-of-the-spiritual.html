About the book

Sayers argues that secularism is giving way not to a return to church but to a revival of magic, from occult influencers to AI cults, and traces that pattern through Scripture, Western history and the present. A Melbourne pastor's sharp cultural read for church leaders wondering why their neighbors suddenly believe in everything.
