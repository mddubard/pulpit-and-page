About the book

Buster traces how biblical apocalyptic shaped C. S. Lewis's fiction, from the Space Trilogy and Narnia to The Great Divorce, his essays, and the notes in his own books' margins. A Wheaton Old Testament scholar's short Hansen Series volume, with responses from John Walton, Esau McCaulley, and Danielle Corple.
