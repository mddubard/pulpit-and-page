Church History

The Reformation & the Reformers

by The Picky Reader

Beyond the introductions. Essential Church History covers the Reformation in outline; this list goes a layer down — the two great Reformers taken seriously as men, the working pastors they trained, and the long argument about what the whole thing finally meant.

Shop these on Bookshop →

Every purchase supports independent bookstores.

1.

Theology of the Reformers

Timothy George · B&H Academic (25th Anniversary ed.)

Luther, Zwingli, Calvin, Simons and Tyndale, each given a full chapter on his own terms. The best single book on what the Reformers actually taught. Buy on Bookshop →

2.

Calvin

Bruce Gordon · Yale University Press

The standard modern life. Gordon refuses both the hagiography and the caricature, and gives you a difficult, brilliant, often unhappy man. Buy on Bookshop →

3.

Luther the Reformer

James M. Kittelson & Hans H. Wiersma · Fortress Press (2nd ed.)

The most balanced one-volume Luther — the career and the theology together, without the psychoanalysis that weighs down other biographies. Buy on Bookshop →

4.

Calvin's Company of Pastors

Scott M. Manetsch · Oxford University Press

What the Reformation looked like as a job. Manetsch works through Geneva's pastoral records to show ordinary ministers doing ordinary work for seventy years. Buy on Bookshop →

5.

Protestants: The Faith That Made the Modern World

Alec Ryrie · Penguin Books

Five centuries and six continents, argued with real verve. Ryrie takes Protestantism seriously as a global movement rather than a European episode. Buy on Bookshop →

6.

The Age of Reform, 1250–1550

Steven Ozment · Yale University Press (40th Anniversary ed.)

The intellectual backdrop. Ozment starts three centuries early, so the Reformation arrives as the end of a long argument rather than a bolt from nowhere. Buy on Bookshop →

7.

John Calvin: A Pilgrim's Life

Herman J. Selderhuis · IVP Academic

Shorter and warmer than Gordon, organised around Calvin's own restlessness. A good first Calvin biography, and a good second one. Buy on Bookshop →
