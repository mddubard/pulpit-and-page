About The Picky Reader

Who we are · What we do

The Picky Reader is a curated bookstore built on a simple conviction: not every book is worthy of shelf space. We read widely and choose carefully, gathering the books worth your time into shelves — each its own corner of the store and its own weekly newsletter. The Theology Shelf came first; the History Shelf is now open, with more on the way.

What you'll find here

A growing library of new and notable releases, browsable by shelf and searchable across the whole store; a weekly Release Calendar of what's new and coming; honest, concise reviews and curated reading lists — the best titles for pastors, seasonal previews, and more; and a used & vintage shelf of out-of-print copies, one of each, shipped from Chattanooga.

Our approach

We recommend across traditions, with a home base in confessional Reformed and broadly evangelical Christianity, and we widen the aisles as new shelves open. We aim to be fair to books we disagree with and honest about the ones we love. Our reviews are our own — never dictated by publishers or swayed by whether a title earns us a commission.

Find us in person

Who's behind it

The Picky Reader is independently written and curated from Chattanooga, Tennessee. Questions, corrections, or a book we should cover? Email info@thepickyreader.com — we read every message.
